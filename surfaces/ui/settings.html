Accounts

Google, Microsoft 365, Apple and others, for the calendar and contacts. Each signs in on its own page; Talaris never sees a password.

Add an account

Google or Microsoft 365: choose it and sign in. Apple: choose WebDAV, with the server https://caldav.icloud.com, your Apple ID, and an app-specific password made at appleid.apple.com.
